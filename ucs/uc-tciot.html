Course Lectures

  • I Foundations IoT reference architecture, the machine-to-machine paradigm and signal acquisition. Lectures 1–2
  • II Long-Range Connectivity Low-power wide area networks and the trade-offs between range, autonomy and cost. Lectures 3
  • III Short-Range Connectivity Bluetooth and BLE, gateway design and Zigbee mesh networks. Lectures 4–7
  • IV Networking Constrained Devices IPv6 addressing, 6LoWPAN adaptation, 6TiSCH and routing in lossy networks. Lectures 8–10
  • V Application Protocols and Middleware Session-layer protocols and message-oriented middleware for data distribution. Lectures 11–12
  • VI Integration and Assessment End-to-end integration from sensor to broker, and final review. Lectures 13–14
