Infraestruturas de Computação na Nuvem

BSc Course Materials | ESTGA - University of Aveiro

Course Lectures

  • I Foundations of Cloud Infrastructure Datacenter concepts, service and deployment models, Uptime Institute tiers and availability calculations. Lectures 1–2
  • II Virtualization and Containers Hypervisors, KVM and LXC on Proxmox VE, kernel namespaces and cgroups, and the Docker container model. Lectures 3–4
  • III Orchestration and Automation Kubernetes architecture, software-defined networking with OpenFlow and Open vSwitch, and Infrastructure as Code with Ansible and Terraform. Lectures 5–7
  • IV Availability, Scalability and Resilience Auto-scaling, load balancing and zero-downtime deployments, plus redundancy and asynchronous decoupling with message brokers. Lectures 8–9
  • V Storage Systems Local storage with RAID and LVM, and networked/distributed storage with NFS, iSCSI and object storage. Lectures 10–11
  • VI Systems Management Technologies Classic monitoring and management with SNMP, WBEM/CIM and WS-Management, and a general course review. Lectures 12–13
